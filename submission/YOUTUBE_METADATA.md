## Description
Mess food gives most hostel students about 30 g of protein a day. A 68 kg person building muscle needs about 109 g. Protein Recharge works out the exact cheapest way to close that gap, around your mess meals, your kettle and your budget.

▶ Try it free (no sign-up, works on your phone): https://protein-recharge.embarko.app

What it does
• 5 questions: weight, goal, diet, kitchen (none / kettle / full), mess meals
• Daily "recharge packs": each is the exact cheapest mix of foods for that price, found by an optimiser rather than guessed
• Drag the budget and the plate re-optimises live
• Skip or pin any food, set your own shop's prices
• Weekly shopping list in real pack sizes

How I built and shipped it
Built with Claude Code (React + TypeScript). Deployed to Embarko by pasting Embarko's deployment prompt into Claude Code. The deploy in the video is a real one, sped up 3×.

Targets follow ICMR-NIN Dietary Guidelines for Indians (2024). Prices are typical estimates you can edit. This is general information, not medical advice.

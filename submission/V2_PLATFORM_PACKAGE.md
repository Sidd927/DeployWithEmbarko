## Playbook

**Before posting (15 minutes)**
- Set the bio link and bio line. Have the cover images on your phone.
- Watch the video once on your phone **with sound**.
- Check the competition basics: #DeployWithEmbarko and the app link in every primary post, and the posts set to public.
- Post the Instagram Reel when your audience is online (check Insights → Most active times; for Indian students it's usually 7–10 pm IST). Post the Short 15–30 minutes later, then X.

**First hour**
- Reply to **every** genuine comment with something useful, not "thanks!". For example, answer "what about ₹30/day veg?" with the actual plan from the app. That creates real conversation and repeat visits.
- Send the Reel **personally** to 5–10 people who'd genuinely use it (your gym friends, hostel group). Individual messages, not mass blasts.
- Share it to your Story with the poll and link sticker.
- Avoid: engagement pods, "like for like", posting the link in unrelated groups, and buying anything. These are against the competition rules and can't be undone.

**First 24 hours**
- Post in **one or two** places where it's on-topic and allowed: your college's fitness or hostel group, r/developersIndia's showcase thread, r/SideProject. Read each group's rules first, and lead with the fact, not "please like".
- Answer questions in your replies; screenshots of the app answering someone's budget are great Story material.
- If someone asks for a food or price you don't have, note it. That becomes your follow-up content.

**After 24–48 hours: read the numbers**
| Metric (where) | What it tells you | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram: **skip rate / avg watch time** (Reel Insights) | Did the first second hold people? | If most people leave in the first second, the hook isn't landing: try a **Trial Reel** with a different first line (for example "₹53 a day for 79 g of protein") |
| Instagram: **sends** per reach | Is it shareable? | High sends: make a part 2 ("cheapest protein with no kitchen") |
| YouTube: **"Chose to view" %** (Shorts → "How many chose to view") | Hook and frame-0 strength in the feed | Compare with your own channel's Shorts. YouTube publishes no benchmark, so ignore online "70% rules" |
| YouTube: avg % viewed | Pacing | If people drop at the Embarko section, keep it but tighten it in the next cut |
| X: video views, replies | Conversation | Reply to every question; quote-post the best one with an answer |

**Follow-up content, only if v2 performs:**
- "Cheapest protein with **no kitchen**" (switch kitchen to None in the app and show the new plan).
- "Vegetarian ₹50/day plan".
- "I asked the app for ₹30/day".

Each one is a real app interaction, so every follow-up post is also a live demo.

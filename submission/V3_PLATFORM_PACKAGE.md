## Reach strategy (what actually moves these algorithms)
1. **Sends are the signal.** The video ends on "Send this to your gym friend" for that reason. Personally send it to 5–10 people who'd use it (gym friends, hostel group) in the first hour. Use individual messages, not blasts.
2. **Reply to every comment with something useful.** For example, answer "₹30 veg?" with the actual plan from the app. That makes real conversation and repeat visits.
3. **Story:** share the Reel with a **link sticker** to the app and a **poll**: "Daily protein spend? <₹50 / ₹50–150 / ₹150+".
4. **Collab** only with a real friend or club account whose audience cares (your gym club, a hostel page).
5. **One or two on-topic communities** (college fitness or hostel group, r/developersIndia showcase, r/SideProject). Read their rules first and lead with the fact.
6. **After 24–48 h:**
   - If the Instagram skip rate is high, try a **Trial Reel** with a different first line.
   - If YouTube's "chose to view" is low, try another cover frame.
7. **Never:** engagement pods, like-for-like, bought views, or spamming the link. These break the competition rules and can't be undone.

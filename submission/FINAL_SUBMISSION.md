## Description for the form (if a text field is shown)
Protein Recharge helps hostel and PG students in India hit their daily protein on a small budget. You answer five questions (weight, goal, diet, kitchen access and mess meals) and an exact optimiser finds the cheapest foods to close your protein gap. Plans appear as daily "recharge packs" (₹29, ₹49, ₹53…). Drag the budget and the plate re-optimises live. You can skip or pin foods and set your own shop's prices, and it writes a weekly shopping list in real pack sizes. It is free, needs no sign-up and stores data on your device. Built with Claude Code and deployed with Embarko's deployment prompt.

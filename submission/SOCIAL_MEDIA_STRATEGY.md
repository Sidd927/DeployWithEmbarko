# Social Media Strategy

Reach is worth **300 points**, scored on views and engagement visible at judging (about 5 Dec), and posting on each platform adds **+50 points** (+150 for all three). The aim is genuine reach from people who would actually use the app. **No bots, paid boosts, engagement pods, fake accounts or giveaway-for-follows**, all of which are banned by Rules §3 and §5A.

**Submit the entry as soon as the first post is live.** Don't wait for all three, because resubmitting with the same email and phone replaces the entry and the latest one is judged. Then resubmit once all the links exist.

## Getting real people to watch (ranked by expected impact)
1. **Your own hostel and class WhatsApp groups.** These are the exact target users. Send the *app link* with one line: "Made this for us mess-food people: tells you the cheapest way to hit protein. Try your own numbers." People who try it will often watch the reel too.
2. **Your college gym or fitness group and the hostel mess committee.** A real use case, not spam: offer it as a tool.
3. **Instagram Story** with a link sticker to the reel and a poll ("How much protein does your mess give you?"). Polls lift engagement.
4. **Reply to comments within the first hour.** Ask people to "comment your budget and I'll tell you your pack". This is genuine engagement, and the app really answers it.
5. **Tag @embarko** (or Embarko's handle on each platform) and use #DeployWithEmbarko. The sponsor may reshare entries.
6. **Pin a comment** with the app link on Instagram and YouTube, because captions are truncated.
7. Repost the reel to your Story on day 3 with "Updated: you can now set your own shop's prices" (a true feature) to bring back people who missed it.

## What not to do
- Don't buy views, use giveaway loops or ask unrelated groups to "like for like" (disqualification risk).
- Don't post the same link in many unrelated Reddit or Facebook groups. That's spam, and the rules prohibit it.
- Don't claim health outcomes. The app plans protein. It is not medical advice.
- Keep every post **public until the results are announced**.

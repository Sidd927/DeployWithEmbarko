The voice adds what the picture can't: who is speaking, why it matters, and how it feels to use. It never reads a title word for word. The two biggest sound moments, the **"Goal hit" pop at 37.1 s** and the **"Live" pop at 71.5 s**, get silent space.

**Honesty rules:**
- No personal life claims (for example, "I live in a hostel") unless you confirm they're true.
- Product claims match the app: the optimiser is exact for the chosen constraints, and the deploy shown is real, at 3× speed.

Pace target: about 2.6 words a second, with at least 0.2 s of air at each segment's edges. Each segment is generated separately, then placed at its start time in the HyperFrames timeline.

## Pronunciation notes for the clone
- Written so the model reads it correctly: "fifty-three rupees" isn't used (the title shows ₹53). "protein recharge dot embarko dot app" is spelled out.
- Indian food words (dal, roti) appear only once, in segment 2. If the clone mispronounces them, the fallback line is "Mess meals only cover part of it. The rest is a gap."
- "Embarko": stress on the second syllable, em-BAR-ko. If the clone stresses it wrongly, generate that segment again with a different energy.

**Principle:** the big on-screen titles already state the facts (₹50, 79 g, ₹53, "Live in under a minute"). The voice adds what the picture can't: who is speaking, why it matters, and how it feels to use. It never reads a title word for word. The two biggest sound moments, the **"Goal hit" pop at 37.1 s** and the **"Live" pop at 71.5 s**, get silent space.

**Honesty rules:**
- No personal life claims (for example, "I live in a hostel") unless you confirm they're true.
- Product claims match the app: the optimiser is exact for the chosen constraints, and the deploy shown is real, at 3× speed.
- The narration is generated by NarraReel/Chatterbox from your own recording, with your consent. Chatterbox embeds Resemble AI's inaudible Perth watermark, and the posts should say "AI voice clone of my own voice" (see the disclosure note at the end).

Pace target: about 2.6 words a second, with at least 0.2 s of air at each segment's edges. Each segment is generated separately, then placed at its start time in the HyperFrames timeline.

## Accuracy correction
Segments 09 and 10 first said "I copied one prompt… I pasted it into my coding agent". In fact Claude Code fetched Embarko's official prompt from embarko.ai and followed it, and the entrant did not copy or paste it. The lines now say what happened. The prompt-scene subtitle was corrected the same way.

## Changes made after testing the clone
The first generations showed that Chatterbox reliably mispronounced "Claude" (heard as plot, cloud or blog), "Dockerfile" (docket fight) and "weekly" (Wiki), and dropped the first consonant of a sentence-initial "Try". Segments 08, 10, 11 and 12 were reworded so the voice avoids those words. The titles on screen still show "Claude Code" and "Dockerfile", so no information is lost.

## Pronunciation notes for the clone
- Written so the model reads it correctly: "fifty-three rupees" isn't used (the title shows ₹53). "protein recharge dot embarko dot app" is spelled out.
- Indian food words (dal, roti) appear only once, in segment 2. If the clone mispronounces them, the fallback line is "Mess meals only cover part of it. The rest is a gap."
- "Embarko": stress on the second syllable, em-BAR-ko. If the clone stresses it wrongly, generate that segment again with a different energy.

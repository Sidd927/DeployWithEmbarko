# V3 Creative Audit of the Real-Voice v2 (28 Nov 2026)

Reviewed: `protein-recharge-v2-real-voice-9x16.mp4`, 14 frames across the timeline, plus every earlier version, all footage and the voice manifest.

## What hurts it (in order of damage)
1. **Captions cover proof.** The boxed captions at y 1040–1200 sit on top of app data in at least 5 shots: the peanuts row value under "Per rupee…", the tofu/curd values under "Whey is way down…", the nutrition facts under "Skip any food…", a ₹ price under "Then it writes your shopping list", and the AI logos under "I put it online…".
2. **One look for 47 seconds.** Every product shot is the same light-grey phone screen, edge to edge. Story beats and proof beats look identical, so it reads as a screen-recording slideshow.
3. **The payoff frame collides.** The "₹53 a day" chip overlaps "Your daily top-up" at the exact moment of the goal hit.
4. **Frame 0 isn't a hook.** It's an app list with a small chip. The surprising fact is never shown *big*.
5. The Embarko site and terminal shots don't share any visual language with the rest.

## What works (keep)
- The real voice, all 15 lines, with onset-accurate timing.
- The real footage and the honest data (37.1 g vs 4.1 g, ₹53 → 79.1 g, real deploy).
- The story order (fact → problem → build → demo → payoff → ship → CTA).
- The brand system (haldi, ink, Bricolage).

## V3 decisions
| Problem | V3 answer |
|---|---|
| Captions on the UI | A **caption lane on the stage, under the screen card**. Captions can't overlap the app by construction. Small, clean white subtitles with key words in haldi and no boxes |
| Slideshow feel | **Dark ink stage + one persistent screen card** whose content settles in like a feed scroll. Full-screen typographic beats between proof beats change the rhythm |
| Weak frame 0 | A **designed real-data comparison**: "Soya chunks 37 g per ₹10" huge, then the whey row lands with its tiny bar and "9×" |
| Payoff collision | The card frames budget + slider + meter together. Goal hit = **haldi card border flash + chip above the card** (on the stage, never over the UI) |
| Mixed visual languages | The site and app shots live in the same card. The terminal shot goes full-bleed on the ink stage (same colour family) |
| Length | Your voice sets it: about 45 s with tightened gaps (still ≥ 40 s) |

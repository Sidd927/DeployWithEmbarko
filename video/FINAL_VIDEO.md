## How it meets the judging criteria
- **Opening hook (first 3 s):** frame 0 already shows "₹50 a day. Mess food." over the live voucher, and the price climbs within a second. There is no logo intro.
- **Easy to follow:** one question (enough protein on a budget?), one profile, and the same numbers from start to finish (109 g goal, 30 g from meals, 79 g gap, ₹53/day). Titles are at most 6 words.
- **Storytelling:** need → gap → reveal → setup → packs → payoff (goal hit) → personalise → shopping list → how it went live → URL.
- **Shows Embarko:** 20 s on Embarko. The real embarko.ai homepage with its "Copy deployment prompt" button, then a **real deploy recorded as it ran** (tar → `POST ship.embarko.ai/apps` → building → `live (success)`), then the `*.embarko.app` URL on the end card.

## Authenticity statement
- Every app frame is the live app at protein-recharge.embarko.app, captured in Chrome at 390 × 844 @3×. Embarko's temporary "will be deleted in N hours" banner (unclaimed apps only) was hidden in product shots. Nothing else was altered, and Embarko's badge is visible.
- The deploy segment is a real redeploy executed during recording on 24 Nov 2026. The responses are logged in `video/footage/deploy-log.jsonl`. It is shown at 3× speed and labelled as such on screen.
- No fake UI, stock footage or AI-generated imagery.

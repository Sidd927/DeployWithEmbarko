# Storylines (picked: A)

**A. "₹50 a day: enough protein?" (recommended, picked).** Device: a question with the input. Form: one home stage. The phone window stays in the same place while its contents change, and big titles above it carry the story. A hostel student's real numbers (109 g goal, 30 g from mess, 79 g gap) travel from the hook to the payoff (goal hit at ₹53 a day, the real optimiser output). The film then shows the real Embarko prompt and the real deploy.
- First 2 s: "₹50 a day. Mess food." printed over the live voucher, with its price climbing as a finger drags the budget.
- Beats: hook → gap (real equation) → reveal (Protein Recharge on haldi) → 5 questions → packs → slider payoff → exclude soya → list → Embarko prompt → real deploy → end card URL.
- Look: the app's own system, steel ground, haldi voucher, Bricolage Grotesque + Figtree.
- Sound: bundled UI effects on visible actions, a riser cresting at "Goal hit". Music bed to add if a catalog sign-in is available.

**B. "Protein per ₹10" (type carries the argument).** Giant kinetic numbers (37.1 g per ₹10 for soya against 7.8 g for peanut butter) cut on the beat, then the app turns the ranking into a plan.

**C. "The shopping list first" (result first).** Opens on a finished weekly list (₹ total), then pulls back to show the plan, the slider and the profile that produced it.
